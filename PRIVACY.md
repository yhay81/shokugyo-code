# Privacy

- Search terms, major category, and hierarchy filter stay in the browser.
- Compared entries contain only public classification codes and are limited to four records in local storage.
- Telemetry stores only an allowlisted event name, one-way session hash, QA flag, and timestamp.
- Job descriptions, selected codes, contact data, advertising identifiers, and authentication data are not collected.
- Telemetry expires after 35 days; automated QA is excluded from human metrics.
