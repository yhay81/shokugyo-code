# Decisions

## Compare ambiguous work, not just titles

Classification depends on actual work. The product searches official descriptions and lets users place four candidates on one workbench before opening official examples and non-matching examples.

## Preserve the classification boundary

The statistical standard and the Hello Work classification are different systems. The warning is shown near the primary workflow and on the source page. No crosswalk is invented.

## Static local search

All 415 codes fit in one cacheable file. Keeping search local avoids collecting job descriptions and makes query feedback immediate.

## No authentication

The workbench stores four public codes in local storage. Accounts would add friction and privacy risk without improving the core task.
